\chapter{Use of AI Tools}\label{app:ai-tools}

During the preparation of this thesis and the associated research scripts, I used OpenAI ChatGPT/Codex and Microsoft Copilot for the following purposes:

\begin{description}
    \item[Program script development:] Assistance with developing and revising scripts used in the research presented in this thesis.
    \item[Text preparation and proofreading:] Assistance with drafting and revising passages, proofreading, and improving the grammar, wording, and clarity of the thesis text. ChatGPT/Codex was also used to translate the English abstract into German.
    \item[Data analysis:] Assistance with developing and implementing data-analysis procedures and the associated scripts.
\end{description}

This statement documents the use of both tools in these activities. I take responsibility for the submitted text, code, analyses, and results, including any AI-assisted content incorporated into this thesis, as stated in the Declaration of Originality.

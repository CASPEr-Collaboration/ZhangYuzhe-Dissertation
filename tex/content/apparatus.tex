\section{Overview}
The apparatus can be organized into three functional blocks: signal generation, signal reception, and initial processing. The first block prepares the spin system, the second converts magnetic response into an electrical signal, and the third filters and digitizes that signal for later analysis.

This structure mirrors the actual workflow of the experiment. The magnet and excitation hardware define the operating point, the pickup and SQUID chain set the sensitivity, and the processing electronics decide how much of the raw signal can be recovered from noise.
